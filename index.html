Privacy Policy for CryptMax Vault

Effective Date: October 26, 2026

CryptMax Vault ("we", "our", or "us") is designed with zero-knowledge, local-first architecture to protect your personal media and documents.

1. Zero-Knowledge Local Storage

CryptMax Vault operates entirely on your local iOS device. We do not store, view, upload, or transmit your encrypted photos, videos, notes, PDFs, or pattern/passcode credentials to external servers. All encryption keys and vault data remain strictly stored on your local device using AES-256-GCM encryption.

2. Information Collection & Usage

Because CryptMax Vault does not require user account registration, we do not collect:

  • Personal Identifiable Information (PII) such as your name, email, or phone number.
  • Vault contents, media, notes, or passcodes.
  • Location data or browsing history.

3. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

Subscription processing and payment transactions for CryptMax Pro are handled securely by Apple Inc. via StoreKit 2 and the App Store. We do not process or store payment cards, banking credentials, or billing addresses.

4. Camera & Photo Library Access

CryptMax Vault requests access to your device's Camera and Photo Library solely to capture or import photos, videos, and documents at your explicit request. Imported files are encrypted locally, and original source files are only removed from your system Photo Library upon your explicit confirmation.
